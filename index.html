"The 14-Day Check Trap" Systemic Banking Loophole Visualizer

How scammers exploit Federal Reserve Regulation CC provisional clearing laws to steal thousands
CRITICAL SYSTEMIC VULNERABILITY

Most victims believe: "If the money appears in my bank account balance, the check must be good." This is false. Federal law requires banks to make funds available within 24–48 hours, but back-end reconciliation through the Federal Reserve takes up to 14 business days. Scammers exploit this gap:

DAY 1: THE BAIT
Counterfeit Check Received

Victim receives fake corporate cashier's check ($4,850) to buy equipment from an "approved supplier".

Victim Balance: $0.00
DAY 2: THE TRAP CLOSES
Provisional Credit Made

Under federal Reg CC, bank makes provisional credit available. Victim assumes funds are genuine and cleared.

Apparent Balance: +$4,850.00
DAY 3: IRREVERSIBLE WIRE
Zelle / Wire Payment Sent

Victim wires $4,200 out-of-pocket to the scammer's vendor account via irreversible payment.

Liquid Cash Lost: -$4,200.00
DAY 10-12: FED CLEARING
Routing Number Bounces

The issuing bank notifies the clearinghouse that the check routing or signature is fraudulent. The check is revoked.

Bank Status: CHECK BOUNCED
DAY 14: CLAWBACK
Bank Reverses Full Amount

Bank claws back the original $4,850. The victim is left thousands in debt with a frozen bank account.

Victim Net Deficit: -$4,200.00 (DEBT)
💡
Federal Regulatory Gap (12 CFR Part 229 - Expedited Funds Availability Act): Banks are legally prohibited from holding cashier's check funds past the 2nd business day, but have zero responsibility to absorb losses when checks bounce weeks later. SentinelScan detects this pattern before any deposit is made.
